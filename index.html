Meet Vicki

Experience behind the tools.
A person on your side.

Hi, I’m Vicki—the person behind Bravic Enterprises.

Let’s talk about your business →

I help small businesses build better websites, simplify their systems and make everyday administration easier. I enjoy getting to know how a business works, finding what’s taking up unnecessary time, and creating practical tools that make a difference.

I bring more than 30 years of administration and bookkeeping experience, including over 15 years working with my preferred accounting software, Xero. For more than 20 years, I’ve also been building database systems—from Microsoft Access and Microsoft Dynamics through to today’s platforms, including monday.com and Shopify.

My work has taken me across Australia and the USA, where I’ve operated my own database development businesses. I’ve built websites using GoDaddy, WordPress and WooCommerce, and have spent the past two years working with Shopify.

I also own and run Camping Mat Warehouse, so I understand the day-to-day demands of an ecommerce business firsthand. My particular strength is connecting systems and automating the movement of information, helping reduce double handling and keep things organised.

Through Bravic, I’m bringing that experience to other business owners. I enjoy solving problems, designing useful tools and sharing what I’ve learned—while continuing to learn along the way.
